# FirstKnock: First 30 Days of Instagram Content

## Sprint contract

- **Publishing window:** August 3–28, 2026
- **Final seven-day snapshot:** September 4, 2026
- **Cadence:** five primary assets per week
- **Campaign:** `1000-users`
- **Landing page:** `https://firstknock.online/instagram`

Tracked-link template:

```text
https://firstknock.online/instagram?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=1000-users&utm_content={content_id}
```

Before publishing, generate the link in `/GrowthDashboard`; do not type UTM values
manually. Keep the persistent profile link on `utm_content=ig-bio`. Attribute an
asset-specific visit through that asset's Story sticker or a manual DM reply containing
its own link.

For a feed asset followed by a Story, keep the feed asset's content ID. Record feed reach,
views, shares, and saves from the primary asset, and link clicks from its Story sticker.
Do not add Story reach to feed reach. For keyword CTAs, count only relevant human
requests as qualified DM intents.

Use real product screens in a synthetic workspace. Redact names, addresses, contact
details, and invite codes. Label hypothetical rescue scenarios. Do not claim customer
results, exact time savings, conversion lifts, or pricing.

Save the hook and hypothesis before publishing. Enter the cumulative Instagram Insights
snapshot when each asset reaches seven days. Change one major variable at a time.

Planning gates are FirstKnock hypotheses, not Instagram benchmarks:

| Stage | Initial gate |
|---|---:|
| Owned intent / reach | 0.6% |
| Manager signup / owned intent | 25% |
| Activated workspace / manager signup | 35% |
| Activated users / workspace | Directionally toward 5 |

Judge shares and saves against the median of at least three comparable assets.

## Week 1: Make the routing problem visible

**Question:** Which manager pain creates the strongest owned intent?

### 1. The cost of overlapping canvassers

- **Publish / ID:** August 3 — `ig-20260803-01`
- **Format:** Reel, 25–30 seconds
- **Audience / pain:** A door-to-door manager whose reps can be sent into the same streets.
- **Hook:** “Two good reps can still waste the same block.”
- **Script:** “Two reps can work hard and still start on the same streets when territory
  lives in a group chat or a marked-up screenshot. Watch the handoff instead: the manager
  builds the routes, gives each route an owner, and each rep opens assigned work on the
  phone. The point is clear ownership before the first knock. Tap ROUTE in today's Story
  to see the FirstKnock workspace.”
- **Visuals:** Messy overlapping lines on a generic map → route list → assignment control
  → rep route view.
- **One CTA / link:** Story sticker `ROUTE`; use this asset's content ID.
- **Primary success metric:** Landing sessions / Reel reach.
- **Hypothesis:** Explicit overlap pain will meet or beat the 0.6% owned-intent gate.
